AIME answers are integers from 000 to 999.

When solving, follow these rules strictly:

1. **Read the problem twice.** Before solving, restate in your own words exactly what quantity the problem asks for. Common traps:
   - "remainder when divided by 1000" means compute mod 1000
   - "p+q where p/q is in lowest terms" means simplify the fraction FIRST
   - "how many" means count carefully — consider overcounting/undercounting

2. **Show all work.** Never skip a computation step. When multiplying numbers with 3+ digits, break into partial products. When doing modular arithmetic, reduce at each step.

3. **Verify with a different method.** After reaching your answer, check it independently:
   - Substitute back into the original conditions
   - Try small cases to confirm your formula
   - Use a completely different approach if possible
   - At minimum, recompute your final arithmetic step

4. **Confirm your answer is in [0, 999].** If it's not, you made an error — go back and fix it.
